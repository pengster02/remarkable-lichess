The 12 SVGs in this directory (wK, wQ, wR, wB, wN, wP, bK, bQ, bR, bB, bN, bP)
are the "cburnett" chess piece set by Colin M.L. Burnett
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett), as shipped by Lichess itself
at public/piece/cburnett in lichess-org/lila -- confirmed against lila's own
COPYING.md, which lists that directory under GPLv2+.
